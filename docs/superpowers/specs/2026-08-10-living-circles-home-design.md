# Living Circles Home Design

**Status:** Approved for visual creation
**Date:** August 10, 2026

## Purpose

Redesign the Circles screen as Potluck's friendly people-first home. The screen must help a person recognize their groups, see immediate actionable alerts, open a current Circle, or start a new Circle without resembling a finance dashboard.

## Approved direction

The selected direction is **Living Circles**. Circles remains the centered default destination in the persistent `Cards · Circles · Bills` navigation.

### Header

- Use the standalone greeting `Good morning, Joseph`.
- Do not add a descriptive subtitle beneath the greeting.
- Keep the profile control at top right with a visible, accessible private-account attention badge when required.

### Needs attention

- Show one compact `Needs attention` section only when action is required.
- The first item identifies the affected plan, person, Circle, and exact next action in plain language.
- Approved example:
  - Title: `Internet bill needs your review`
  - Detail: `Maya updated her part · Apartment crew`
  - Action: `Review update`
- `See all` reveals additional unresolved actions.
- Do not mix resolved activity or marketing messages into this section.

### Your circles

- Make Circle cards the dominant visual content.
- Make each full card tappable; do not require a small `Open` link.
- Each card shows its Circle name, overlapping member avatars, a concise asset count, and a useful text status.
- Approved examples:
  - `Apartment crew` — `4 people · 2 shared bills` — `Internet bill is almost ready` — `1 action`
  - `Family circle` — `5 people · 1 shared card` — `Everything is on track`
- Do not use a decorative face without adjacent explanatory status text.

### Primary action and navigation

- Replace the ambiguous floating plus on this screen with a wide labeled `Start a circle` button above the bottom navigation.
- Keep the bottom navigation visible and ordered `Cards · Circles · Bills`, with Circles centered and selected.
- Invitations and alerts deep-link to the exact affected item.

## Visual system

- Warm cream background, white content cards, deep teal headings and primary controls.
- Coral is reserved for friendly emphasis and the `Start a circle` action.
- Butter yellow, lavender, and mint support alert and Circle identity without carrying meaning alone.
- Use generous rounded corners, quiet borders, minimal shadow, and clear separation between sections.
- Use the existing Potluck type direction: Epilogue for headings and Manrope for body copy and labels.

## Accessibility requirements

- Minimum 44 by 44 pixel interactive targets.
- Full-row targets for alert and Circle cards.
- Strong contrast for all essential text and controls.
- Status always combines words with an icon or shape; color never carries meaning alone.
- Navigation icons always have visible labels.
- The screen must remain understandable with larger text and dynamic reflow.
- No transient toast may be the only presentation of an important alert.

## Product and marketability gate

- **Target user:** Friends, roommates, families, couples, and mixed-age households coordinating repeated shared expenses.
- **Promise:** See your people, know what needs you, and keep shared plans moving from one welcoming home.
- **Acquisition:** Starting a Circle creates a natural invitation loop around a real shared arrangement.
- **Plan placement:** Circles, essential alerts, accurate statuses, and Circle creation belong to the ungated core.
- **Conversion:** Future paid value may add scale and automation, but this screen must not gate safety, consent, status, or access.
- **Retention:** Reusable Circles and recurring shared plans make the home useful each cycle.
- **Differentiation:** Potluck combines social groups, accepted contribution terms, and readiness rather than acting as a generic bill tracker or reimbursement ledger.
- **Primary metric:** Circle members who return and complete the next required action without Host follow-up.

## Scope

This task creates a high-fidelity mobile visual reference. It does not implement financial behavior, provider calls, money movement, contribution changes, or new application logic.
